NOIR CHARACTER STUDIO — DISTRIBUTION NOTICE

Project material and reference image
------------------------------------
The project's original source code, procedural 3D model, and animations are
released under the MIT license. The full project license is included here as
project-LICENSE.txt and in the source distribution as LICENSE.

The character reference image is user-provided comparison material. It is not
covered by the project's MIT grant, and its inclusion does not assert a separate
license grant for that image. The third-party licenses below remain independent
and apply to their corresponding components.

bundled in this distribution; system fonts provide fallbacks.

The MediaPipe assets also retain their license beside the runtime and model in
the mediapipe directory. Its assets.json lists the official upstream sources
and integrity hashes. No game character assets, game animation files, or music
are bundled.
